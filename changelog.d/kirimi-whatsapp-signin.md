### Added

- Universal vault. Tick Universal on a Vault login, card, contact or API key and it syncs to every computer signed in to your account. Items are encrypted on the computer with a passphrase-protected key before upload, so the account service never sees them or the passphrase. Unsharing can leave each computer's copy or stop syncing on one computer only.
- Universal sync for MCP servers and skills. Turn on Universal for an MCP server or a skill and it follows your account, on every computer or only the computers you pick. Each computer has a name you can change in the Universal vault section, where you can also remove old computers and remove the whole Universal vault. The Universal checkbox is now in the add dialog of a Vault item too. OAuth sign-ins, working directories and agent scope never sync.
- Owner-managed accounts and closed registration. When the account service has `OWNER_WHATSAPP_NUMBERS` set, only an owner number, a number that already has an account, or a number with a pending invitation can start sign-in. Other numbers get the message "This number has no account. Ask the owner to add it." Owners get an Accounts tab in Settings, on desktop and on the web, to create accounts by WhatsApp number and see the list. Without the setting, registration stays open.

- Organizations on the account service. An owner creates an organization and names its first owner by WhatsApp number. Owners and admins add members by number, and each member has one role per organization. An organization has its own vault, encrypted on the server with the `ORG_VAULT_KEY` secret (without it the vault routes answer 503 `org_vault_not_configured`). Owners and admins choose which items are shared with the team.
- Organizations in the app. Settings has a new Organizations section to see your organizations, manage members and roles, and choose which Vault items each organization shares. Shared items appear in your Vault with the organization's name, are read-only there, and agents and tools can use them; they are never copied into your personal Vault and are removed when you leave or sign out. The account service now also reports whether you are a platform owner when listing organizations.

- Project boards. A Boards page in the page rail holds one kanban board per project, kept on the server and shared live by every member and device. Cards have an assignee (a member or an agent), a due date, labels and an optional linked agent thread. Drag cards between columns with the mouse or a finger, or use the card menu to move them with the keyboard. Agents can list boards and create, update and move cards. Assigning a card to an agent does not start a run. Available on desktop and the web, not on mobile.

### Changed

- Sign-in and server invitations now use a WhatsApp number instead of an email address. With reverse sign-in, a person signs in by sending a prepared WhatsApp message to the business number.
- A server allows up to 50 members, with no plan or licence check. An operator can set another limit for one server.
- The account service keeps avatars, marketplace bundles, share cards, host logos and vault blobs in one S3-compatible bucket (AWS S3, Backblaze B2, MinIO, Wasabi and others) instead of two Cloudflare R2 buckets. Set `STORAGE_S3_ENDPOINT`, `STORAGE_S3_BUCKET`, `STORAGE_S3_REGION` and `STORAGE_S3_FORCE_PATH_STYLE`, and the secrets `STORAGE_S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID` and `STORAGE_S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`. Files are stored under the `avatars/` and `skills/` key prefixes. Without these settings, the routes that need storage answer 503 `storage_not_configured`. Sign-in and the other routes keep working.

### Removed

- Company sign-in (single sign-on and SCIM) is removed.
- The invite-a-friend referral programme is removed: invite links, referral credit, the Invite friends settings tab and the referral landing page.
- OpenBot credits are removed: buying, moving and spending them, the Credits page and rail button, and the OpenBot image and video models that they paid for. Sunra, Higgsfield and agent provider media keep working with their own accounts.
- Hosted servers and hosted sites are removed: the cloud servers that OpenBot rented by plan, publishing sites to OpenBot hosting, the site publish tool, and their pricing page, settings and status entries. Self-hosted servers, joining by invitation, and local site preview stay.
- Licences, billing and plans are removed: commercial licences, Stripe checkout and the customer portal, server and storage plans, the Billing and Licence settings, the pricing and checkout pages, and every prompt to pay, upgrade or add seats.
- A server can be attached to an organization (Settings, Organizations, Servers). Its members then join it without an invitation, within the server member limit, and anyone who leaves the organization or is removed from it loses their membership on its servers, except servers they own. Detaching a server or deleting the organization removes nobody. This adds the `0074_organization_servers` migration.
- Internal: the account service deploys to this team's own domain.
- The app, installer and site are called Jujurly: the name comes from one constant and every "OpenBot" in the catalogs renders as "Jujurly", the app and installer use the new name, icon and application id, the owl is the app logo, and the site shows it. Automatic updates are off until an own release location exists, and the download page says the apps are coming soon.
- The app, the browser extension and the account service point at `https://jujurly.id` (one constant, `ACCOUNT_SERVICE_ORIGIN`), and usage and error reporting to an analytics host is switched off (`ANALYTICS_ENABLED`).
