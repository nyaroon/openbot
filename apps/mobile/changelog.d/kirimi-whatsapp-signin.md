### Changed

- Sign-in and server invitations now use a WhatsApp number instead of an email address. With reverse sign-in, you sign in by sending a prepared WhatsApp message.
- A server allows up to 50 members, with no plan or licence check.

### Removed

- Company sign-in (single sign-on and SCIM) is removed.
- The invite-a-friend referral programme is removed, including the Invite friends screen and the invite code field at sign-in.
- OpenBot credits are removed, with the Credits screens and the OpenBot image and video models that they paid for.
- Hosted servers are removed: the cloud server option when you add a server, the plans and sleeping-server screens, and the sites list. Self-hosted servers, joining by invitation, and scanning an invite stay.
- Licences, billing and plans are removed, including the Billing screens and every prompt to pay, upgrade or add seats.
